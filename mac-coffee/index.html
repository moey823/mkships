Native macOS app · macOS 14+ · Apple Silicon

Three simple controls

Stay awake for as long as you need.

Choose what should stay awake and when the session should end.

Keep Awake

Start a keep-awake session from the menu bar. For lid-closed sessions on a MacBook, enable the included helper and approve it in macOS Login Items before starting. Desktop Macs use the standard macOS caffeinate mechanism.

Keep Screen On

Choose Keep Screen On before a session or while one is running. It prevents the display from sleeping while your Mac is awake; it cannot light a MacBook display while the lid is physically closed.

Timer

Choose 1, 15, 30, 60, or 120 minutes—or keep a session running until you stop it. When the timer ends, the active keep-awake session ends too.

Starts off

You're always in control.

Sessions start off each time Mac Coffee launches. Choose to keep your Mac awake only when you need it.

Private by design

Just a local utility.

No account, network connection, or telemetry. Mac Coffee runs on your Mac and keeps its controls close at hand.
