Description of Service

Asterism (listed on the App Store as “Asterism: Life in Weeks”) is an iPhone app that draws your life as a grid of weeks based on a birthdate and a chosen horizon, and lets you mark and revisit the moments that mattered. The App runs entirely on your device. There is no server-side component, no user account, and no cloud storage.

Asterism draws a grid from a life-expectancy estimate you choose (80, 90, or 100 years). It is a reflective tool, not a prediction. It makes no claim about how long you or anyone else will live.

Nature of the Horizon

The grid's length is set by the horizon you pick in Settings — 80, 90, or 100 years — not by any calculation about you. It is a fixed frame for reflection, described in the App itself as “an estimate, not a promise.” Asterism does not diagnose, predict, or provide medical, psychological, financial, or actuarial advice, and nothing in it should be treated as such.
