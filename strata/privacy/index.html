Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Strata does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. All game data (stats, settings, saved games) is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts.

Data Collection

Strata collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no email collection, and no user identifiers of any kind.

Local Storage

Your game statistics, settings, and saved game state are stored on-device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Analytics & Tracking

Strata does not use any analytics services, tracking pixels, crash reporters, or telemetry of any kind. Your gameplay is entirely private.

Third-Party Services

Strata does not integrate with any third-party services. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no external network requests.

Children's Privacy

Since Strata does not collect any data, it does not collect data from children. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date. Since we collect no data, changes are unlikely.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at the app's App Store listing.
