Description of Service

Fuel Gauge (listed on the App Store as “Boat Fuel Gauge & Timer”) is an iOS and Apple Watch utility that estimates remaining fuel by timing engine run sessions and learning a gallons-per-hour burn rate from your fill-ups. It can also record a GPS track of a run. The App processes everything locally on your device. There is no server-side component, no user accounts, and no cloud storage.

The App provides an approximate estimate, not a measurement from a fuel sensor. Do not rely on it as your sole means of knowing how much fuel remains. Always carry an adequate fuel reserve, verify your actual fuel level, and follow safe boating practices.

Nature of the Fuel Estimate

The App computes fuel left as: tank size − (engine run time × gallons-per-hour). This is an estimate that depends on the accuracy of the tank size and burn rate you provide or that the App learns, and on the assumption that your engine's consumption tracks run time. Real consumption varies with throttle, load, current, sea state, and engine condition. The App displays time-remaining as a range to reflect this uncertainty.

You are solely responsible for fuel planning and for ensuring you have enough fuel for any trip. Never use the App as a navigation or safety device.
