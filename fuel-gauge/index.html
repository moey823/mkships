How accurate is it?

It's an estimate, not a sensor. Fuel left = tank size − (run time × gallons-per-hour). Because an outboard's burn changes with throttle, the time-remaining is shown as a range, not a single number.

Treat it as a dependable backup gauge and planning tool — not a replacement for keeping an eye on your actual tank. Always carry an adequate fuel reserve and follow safe boating practices.
