Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect with my partner?

After creating your account, you can invite your partner by sharing your unique invite code. Your partner can enter this code in the app to link your accounts together.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from the Settings section within the app. Alternatively, you can email us at support@couplesdo.com and we will process your request within 48 hours.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is only shared with your connected partner as part of the app's features. We never sell your personal information to third parties. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.

What devices are supported?

CouplesDo is available on iOS and Android devices. You can download it from the App Store or Google Play.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

We appreciate bug reports! Please email us at support@couplesdo.com with a description of the issue, your device type, and any screenshots if possible.
