Privacy policy

Effective September 16, 2026

Your Health readings and personal history are processed on your devices. Attune does not send them to us, sell them, or use them for advertising. The app has no account system, advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

Attune is provided by Matthew Kearney. This policy covers the Attune iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch apps, these Attune web pages, and information you choose to send us for support.

Apple Health access is optional

With your permission, Attune reads heart rate variability (HRV, using Apple Health’s SDNN measurement) and its dates and identifiers. It uses these readings to show your history, calculate personal baselines and trends, compare days with your tags, and find readings near a breathing practice. These calculations happen on your device.

If you separately allow Mindfulness writing, completed breathing practice can be saved to Apple Health as Mindfulness sessions. The app saves active practice time, excluding pauses. The Watch companion asks to write Mindfulness sessions; it does not request HRV read access.

Attune does not use Health data for advertising, marketing, data brokerage, or third-party analytics. We do not receive your Health data. The app does not read sleep records; its Nighttime view is a clock-based window from midnight to 8 a.m., not sleep detection.

You can review or revoke permissions in Apple Health or your device’s Health settings. HRV reading and Mindfulness writing are separate permissions. Revoking access stops future access but does not erase data already saved in Apple Health or local copies previously retained by Attune. Breathing practice remains available without Health access.

What stays on your devices

Attune stores records needed to provide its features:

  • Practice history: session identifiers, dates, active duration, selected pace, source device, and Health save status. When an HRV reading matches a practice, its value, date, and identifier may be retained with that practice.
  • Your context: daily tags and notes you enter.
  • Preferences: onboarding status, baseline window, breathing duration, pace, visual guide, sound, haptics, and reminder settings.
  • Local housekeeping: export dates, filenames, formats, and item counts, plus a deletion date and count used to prevent old Watch sessions from being restored after a local reset.

The Apple Watch app also keeps preferences, an unfinished-practice checkpoint, and completed sessions waiting to reach your iPhone. App data uses storage provided by the operating system. Attune does not operate an account-based cloud service or a developer-hosted copy of your history.

Transfers between your Watch and iPhone

When you finish a Watch practice, Apple’s WatchConnectivity service transfers its session identifier, start and end dates, active duration, pace, and Mindfulness save status to the paired iPhone. Pending transfers can remain on your Watch until delivery succeeds. They are not sent to a server operated by us.

Apple Health syncing and device backups are separate Apple features controlled by your settings and Apple’s policies. Device backups may include locally stored app data. Attune does not control copies in your Apple backups or Health account.

Reminders

If you enable a daily practice reminder, Attune asks for notification permission and schedules it locally on your device. It does not register you for a developer-operated push notification or marketing service. You can turn reminders off in Attune or device notification settings.

Export, retention, and deletion

Export: Settings → Data lets you save CSV or JSON files. Exports can contain available HRV readings, practice details, tags, notes, preferences, and local export/deletion history. Files leave the app only when you choose a destination or share them. A cloud drive, email provider, or other recipient handles the copy under its own policies.

Retention: Local history is kept until you delete it or remove the app’s data. Attune has no developer-held copy to restore.

Delete local app data: This command in Settings removes the iPhone’s practice records, saved HRV matches, tags, notes, export history, and saved preferences, and resets onboarding. It cancels the app’s scheduled reminder. A local deletion date/count and default preferences remain; the deletion date helps reject older queued Watch sessions.

This reset does not delete records in Apple Health, exports you saved elsewhere, backups, or the Watch app’s preferences, checkpoints, and pending transfers. Manage those copies separately through Apple Health, the relevant file/backup service, or the Watch app’s storage. If Health access remains enabled, Attune may read your Health history again after a reset; revoke that access if you do not want further reads.

There is no Attune account to delete. We cannot remotely view, correct, or erase records stored only on your devices.

Support messages and this website

If you email hello@mkships.app, we receive your email address and whatever message or attachments you choose to provide. We use that information to answer your request and maintain a record of the issue. Our email provider processes the message to deliver and store it. Please avoid sending Health readings, exported history, or private notes unless you decide they are necessary for your request.

Support correspondence is kept while needed to address the issue and related follow-up or to meet legal obligations. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete correspondence we hold; we may retain information where legally required. We do not use support messages for advertising or sell them.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address, to serve and secure the website under its privacy statement. The Attune pages include no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or third-party fonts. Opening website or email links uses your browser or email app and is separate from Attune’s local Health processing.

Apple separately handles App Store transactions, Apple Health services, backups, and optional system diagnostics under its own settings and privacy policies. Attune does not add a third-party crash-reporting service.

Children’s privacy

Attune does not require users, including children, to send us personal information or Health records to use the app. If a child has sent personal information through support and you want it removed, contact us.

Changes to this policy

We will post updates here with a revised effective date. If the app’s data practices change, its in-app and App Store disclosures will be updated as appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests about information you have sent us, contact Matthew Kearney at hello@mkships.app. For help using the app, visit Attune support.
