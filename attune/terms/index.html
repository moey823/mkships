For personal wellbeing, not medical decisions

Attune provides informational views of available Apple Health data and guided breathing. It is not a medical device, diagnostic service, treatment, or emergency monitoring system. Baselines, trends, tag comparisons, and readings near a practice do not establish a diagnosis, a cause, or a promised health outcome.

Do not use Attune as a substitute for professional medical advice. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional about health concerns or medical decisions. Practice in a safe setting, choose a comfortable breathing pace, and stop if you feel unwell. Do not use a breathing exercise while driving or doing something that requires your full attention.

Readings and feature availability

HRV features depend on compatible devices, Apple Health permissions, and available readings. Apple Watch records HRV at intervals; Attune does not take a live HRV measurement or guarantee a reading during or after a practice. An absence of readings can reflect empty history or unavailable access.

The Nighttime filter uses the hours from midnight to 8 a.m. in your current time zone. It does not detect whether you were asleep. Tags and comparisons provide personal context and should not be treated as proof of cause and effect.

Reminders, Watch delivery, and Health saves depend on operating-system permissions and conditions. They are not guaranteed alerts or emergency services. Features may change with app and operating-system updates.

Your records and choices

Your notes and personal records remain yours. You choose whether to grant Health access, save Mindfulness sessions, enable reminders, or export files. The privacy policy explains what is stored, transferred, and retained.

Local records may be lost if you erase app data, remove the app, lose a device, or encounter a device or storage failure. We do not maintain a server copy of your history and cannot recover records stored only on your devices. Export files contain personal information; choose storage and recipients carefully. Attune’s CSV and JSON exports are for viewing or using elsewhere; the current app does not provide an import/restore function.
