Terms of use

Effective September 16, 2026

Attune helps you understand your HRV patterns and make time to breathe. It is an independent app by Matthew Kearney.

For everyday wellbeing

Attune is not a medical device, treatment, or emergency service. Its trends and comparisons do not diagnose conditions, prove causes, or guarantee health outcomes. Ask a qualified professional about health concerns.

Breathe at a comfortable pace and stop if you feel unwell. Practice somewhere safe, never while driving.

What to expect

Health features need your permission and available Apple Health readings. Attune does not measure live HRV or guarantee a reading after practice. Reminders and Watch syncing depend on your devices and settings. Features may change with updates.

Your data is yours

You control Health access, reminders, and exports. See our privacy policy for how data is handled and deleted.

We cannot restore local records lost through deletion or device failure. Exported files may contain sensitive information; choose where you save or share them carefully. Attune cannot import exports to restore your history.
