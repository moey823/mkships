An independent Signal utility

Decrumb is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Signal. It uses the separate, unofficial signal-cli client. Signal’s name and marks belong to their respective owners. Access to Signal’s service remains subject to that service’s terms and availability.

The utility may require updates when Signal changes. It does not guarantee uninterrupted receiving, delivery of every cleaned link, or successful removal of every note.

Use and cleanup limits

Link only an account you are authorized to use. Decrumb’s automation sends only to Note to Self. It is not an emergency or archival messaging system.

Removal requests are limited to eligible, locally recorded sends within the supported window. An acknowledgement does not establish erasure across devices, and a deleted-message marker can remain. Keep independent copies of anything you need before requesting removal.

Local pending notes and Signal state remain on your Mac as described in the privacy policy. Filesystem snapshots, backups, and copies on other devices are outside local cleanup’s control.

Questions or problems

Use GitHub issues for questions and bug reports. The tracker is public, so include only non-sensitive information and synthetic examples. See Help & support for common questions.
