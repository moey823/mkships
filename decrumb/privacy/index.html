Decrumb · Your data

Privacy, without the fine print.

Effective September 20, 2026

Decrumb processes links on your Mac and uses Signal to receive messages and send cleaned notes. There is no Decrumb account, hosted message-processing service, telemetry, advertising, or analytics.

Linking gives the app access to Signal messages

Decrumb acts as an additional linked device through the unofficial signal-cli client. Linking allows that client to receive and decrypt messages for your account, even though Decrumb only processes eligible incoming text for URL cleanup. Signal handles network delivery under its own policies.

Cleaned links are sent only to Note to Self, never to contacts or groups. Optional sender attribution is on by default and uses an available display name; a raw phone number or account identifier is not substituted. Turn attribution off if you do not want names copied into notes.

Decrumb excludes disappearing messages, view-once content, spoilers, edits, story replies, outgoing/synced messages, and unsupported privacy metadata. It does not request read receipts; ordinary Signal delivery receipts still occur.

Ordinary attachments, avatars, stories, and stickers are skipped. The pinned Signal client makes an exception for long-text attachments: it can download them temporarily and reconstruct the message body. Eligible reconstructed text within the app’s size limit can be processed. A hard crash can leave temporary data behind.

Local storage is not zero storage

Runtime data stays in ~/Library/Application Support/SideletLinkCleaner. The internal directory name remains for upgrade compatibility. It is separate from the app bundle and source repository.

  • Queued notes: a plaintext SQLite outbox holds cleaned URLs, the Decrumb code, and optional sender attribution while waiting to send. Decrumb does not keep the original message body in that outbox.
  • Receipt metadata: hashes, the generated note code, send timestamps, deadlines, and delivery/cleanup states let Decrumb identify its own notes. Receipts contain no URLs, message bodies, display names, or raw account identifiers.
  • Signal state: the linked client retains keys, credentials, contacts, groups, profiles, and protocol data. It also has an encrypted incoming-message cache. Processed entries are normally removed, but crashes or trust errors can leave cached envelopes for later handling.
  • Configuration and diagnostics: settings and fixed-event logs remain local. Pairing QR images are temporary and removed when pairing finishes or is cancelled. Diagnostic logs are designed to omit message text, links, contacts, and keys.

Folder permissions limit access; they do not encrypt the outbox or all Signal state. Device access, filesystem snapshots, and backups can expose or retain copies. Protect your Mac accordingly.

How long data remains

Queued payloads are cleared after an attempted send, local cancellation, or expiry. A cancellation before dispatch preserves the unattempted note. Pending payloads become eligible for cleanup after 24 hours; delivery and generated-note receipt metadata after 30 days. Aggregate loss counters persist separately.

Local maintenance runs when the worker operates or during relevant app actions when its lock is available. Data can remain longer while the Mac, app, or worker is stopped or paused without another maintenance action. No disk-deletion deadline can be enforced while the Mac is off.

SQLite secure deletion is enabled, but it does not erase previous snapshots or backups. Pausing preserves valid queued notes unless Discard queued links on pause is enabled. Clear queued links removes only unattempted local notes.

Notes on your Signal devices have a separate lifetime

Once sent, a cleaned note can be stored on your Signal devices. Manual cleanup is the default; optional lifetimes or sweeps request removal only for eligible sends recorded by this installation and bound to the linked account.

Signal’s supported removal window is 24 hours and deletion is best effort. An accepted request is not proof of erasure on every device. Deleted-message markers, quotes, exports, and backups can remain. Local outbox cleanup, deleting the original incoming message, or uninstalling the app does not retract delivered notes.

Decrumb does not change your conversation-wide disappearing-message timer to implement its own lifetimes. Notes may still inherit the timer already configured for Note to Self.

To revoke account access, unlink Decrumb’s device from Signal on your phone and stop its local worker. That does not automatically erase local files or delivered notes. See cleanup help for controls and limits.

The website and support are public services

GitHub Pages hosts this website. These pages have no analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Source downloads, releases, and the issue tracker are hosted on GitHub. Issues and their attachments are public. Please use synthetic examples and do not post private messages, real contacts, sensitive URLs, pairing codes, credentials, or your runtime directory.

For a general privacy question, use the issue tracker without including sensitive information. Security vulnerabilities can be reported privately on GitHub. This policy will be updated here if the app’s behavior changes.
