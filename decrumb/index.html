A Signal helper for an always-on Mac, Raspberry Pi, or Umbrel server

Clean links from Signal.
Saved automatically.

Decrumb removes tracking tags from links you receive and saves clean copies to Note to Self—ready to open or share.

Leave it running on a Mac mini, Raspberry Pi, or Umbrel server. Cleaning only works while that machine is awake, online, and running Decrumb’s helper. If it sleeps, cleaning stops.

Find the links on your phone.

Keep using Signal as usual. Decrumb runs on your Mac, Pi, or Umbrel server and sends cleaned links to Note to Self, where you can open them on your phone.

A MacBook works too, while it’s awake and online. A Mac you regularly put to sleep won’t provide continuous cleaning.

  1. Link Signal.Scan Decrumb’s QR code from Signal on your phone.
  2. Cleaning starts automatically.Use the default rules or choose which sites to clean.
  3. Open Note to Self.Your cleaned links appear there automatically.

Clean a link from anywhere.

Turn on optional phone commands, then send /decrumb clean followed by a link to Note to Self. Decrumb applies your rules and replies there. Send /decrumb status to check the helper.
