Small job. Useful controls.

01 · Clean

Just the changed links.

Remove known tracking parameters without opening the link. Decrumb preserves functional parameters and skips recognized signed links.

02 · Choose

Your sites, your rules.

Clean all eligible sites or a selection. Exclude sites, keep specific parameters, and preview changes locally before saving.

03 · Keep tidy

Notes you can manage.

Each note has a searchable Decrumb code. Keep notes, adjust individual lifetimes, or request removal of eligible notes the app recorded sending.

A linked device on your Mac

A helper for your existing Signal.

Connect from Signal’s Linked Devices screen, choose your rules, and turn cleaning on. Decrumb processes fresh incoming text and saves only links it changes.

It sends only to Note to Self. It never replies to a contact or group, and it ignores its own notes to avoid loops.

Cleanup is optional

Keep by default. Remove when you choose.

Removal is manual by default. Optional lifetimes and periodic sweeps can request removal of tracked notes within Signal’s supported 24-hour window.

Removal is best effort: a deleted-message marker may remain, and an acknowledgement does not prove every device erased the note. Automatic cleanup needs the worker running on an awake, online Mac.

How note cleanup works →

No Decrumb cloud or telemetry

The work happens on your Mac.

The app uses Signal to receive messages and send your notes. There is no Decrumb account, hosted message-processing service, advertising, or analytics.

Local storage still matters: queued links and optional sender names are held in a plaintext outbox until attempted or cleared. Signal’s linked-device keys and state also stay on your Mac.
