Get Decrumb.

Clean the links you receive on Signal and save them to Note to Self automatically.

Still in testing. What’s being tested →

Three steps to get started.

  1. Install Decrumb. Open the download and drag Decrumb into Applications.
  2. Connect Signal. Open Decrumb and choose Connect Signal. On your phone, go to Signal → Settings → Linked Devices → Link a New Device, then scan the QR code.
  3. Turn on cleaning. Choose your rules and select Resume cleaning. Cleaned links will appear in Note to Self.

You’ll need an existing Signal account. Your Mac must stay awake and online while cleaning runs. Intel Macs aren’t supported.

Need a hand? →

Already have Decrumb?

Choose Check for Updates… in the app. Automatic updates are optional and off by default.

Updating from RC1?

If you enabled Discard queued links on pause, turn it off first to keep pending links.

Pause cleaning and quit Decrumb. Download this version, drag it into Applications, and choose Replace. Reopen Decrumb and resume cleaning. Your Signal connection and saved settings stay in place.

RC1 needs this manual update once; future updates can happen in the app.
