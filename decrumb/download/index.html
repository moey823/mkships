Everything needed is included.

Decrumb includes its worker, URL cleaner, and pinned unofficial Signal CLI. You do not need Homebrew, Python, Java, or a separate Signal CLI installation.

  • An Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26.4 or newer.
  • An existing Signal account on your phone.
  • An awake, online Mac while you are logged in for background cleaning.

This download is for Apple silicon; an Intel build is not available.

Install and connect.

  1. Open the downloaded DMG and drag Decrumb to Applications.
  2. Open Decrumb from Applications and choose Connect Signal.
  3. On your phone, open Signal → Settings → Linked Devices → Link a New Device, then scan the QR code.
  4. Choose your cleaning rules and resume cleaning.

Updates, on your terms.

This release includes verified in-app updates. Choose Check for Updates… from the menu, or enable automatic checks and installation in App updates. Both options start off. Updates preserve your linked account, rules, queued work, receipts, and cleaning preference.

RC1/build 1 users need to install this DMG once to enable future in-app updates. No relinking is required.
