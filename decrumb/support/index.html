Set up Signal.

How do I connect?

Install Decrumb, open it, and choose Connect Signal. On your phone, go to Signal → Settings → Linked Devices → Link a New Device, then scan Decrumb’s QR code. If it expires, generate a new one.

Decrumb becomes another linked device. Keep using Signal on your phone as usual. Cleaning starts automatically after connecting. You can change the rules at any time.

Does my Mac need to stay on? Can I use a MacBook?

Yes—Decrumb’s helper needs its Mac awake, online, and logged in, with cleaning enabled. An always-on Mac, such as a Mac mini, is the best fit for continuous cleaning. Its screen can be off.

A MacBook works while it’s awake and online. When it sleeps, goes offline, or shuts down, Decrumb stops cleaning. The helper runs on that Mac, so using Signal on your phone doesn’t keep it running.

Decrumb can keep cleaning after you close its window or quit its menu bar interface. Pause cleaning stops it until you reopen Decrumb or choose Resume cleaning.

Enable Start Decrumb at login to open it automatically when you log in. This does not keep your Mac awake.

Is this an official Signal app?

No. Decrumb is an independent, open-source app that connects through the unofficial signal-cli client. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Signal.

Can I use a Raspberry Pi?

Yes—the Pi edition targets Raspberry Pi OS 64-bit, Bookworm or later. It runs as a background service with a command-line interface and terminal QR pairing. Keep it powered on and online. Enable boot startup as described in the Pi guide. It is an early release; physical Pi and live Signal acceptance testing remain.

Find your cleaned links.

Where do the links go?

Open Note to Self in Signal. Decrumb sends only to that chat, never to your contacts or groups.

Notes include a searchable Decrumb code and, by default, the sender’s available display name. You can turn sender names off. A display name is not a verified identity.

Why wasn’t a link saved?

Decrumb saves a note only when it removes tracking tags. A link may already be clean, fall outside your chosen sites, or be protected by a keep rule or a recognized signature.

It skips disappearing messages, view-once content, spoilers, edits, story replies, ordinary outgoing or synced messages, and bare links in Note to Self. Optional phone commands are the explicit exception. It also skips messages from before cleaning first started, messages over 24 hours old, and messages with unsupported privacy information.

Check your rules and try a made-up example in Try a link. Heavy bursts or uncertain sends can still lose work; Decrumb is not a message archive.

Does Try a link open or send the link?

No. It shows what your draft rules would change, locally on your Mac. It does not open, send, or save your sample. Background cleaning doesn’t visit or expand links either.

Use Decrumb from your phone.

Use RC5 or later for phone commands. RC5 fixes a problem that could make valid Note to Self commands go unanswered. After updating, send the command again.

What can I send to Note to Self?

On Mac, open Cleaning rules, turn on Enable commands from Note to Self, and save. On Pi, run decrumb phone-commands enable on your Pi. The feature is off by default.

  • /decrumb clean followed by a link: apply your current cleaning rules and reply with the result.
  • /decrumb status: check whether the helper received your request and see queue counts.
  • /decrumb help: show the command list.

Only your own self-to-self commands are accepted. The helper must be running and online; commands sent while it was stopped are skipped. Disappearing, view-once, spoiler, group and edited commands are excluded. Replies follow your note-cleanup settings.

These commands cannot execute programs, read files, open links, or call an AI provider.

Keep notes or remove them.

Will notes delete themselves?

Only if you enable automatic cleanup. Removal is manual by default.

You can give new notes a lifetime of 1, 6, 12, or 23 hours, or schedule cleanup every 1, 6, or 12 hours. Each note can have its own lifetime. Choose Keep to cancel its deadline and exclude it from scheduled cleanup; you can still request manual removal.

Changing the default lifetime affects new notes. Existing deadlines stay until you change them.

Which notes can Decrumb remove?

Only notes this installation recorded successfully sending to your linked account. Your personal notes and notes without that local record are left alone. A matching link, name, or Decrumb code is not enough.

Removal is limited to eligible notes sent within the last 24 hours. Older notes, uncertain sends, or missing records may need manual removal in Signal.

Does “Removal requested” mean it’s gone everywhere?

No. Signal’s client accepted the request, but deletion is best effort. Decrumb stops requests shortly before the 24-hour limit to leave time for sending them.

A deleted-message marker may remain, as can quotes, saved copies, and backups. Deleting the original incoming message does not delete Decrumb’s note.

What if my Mac sleeps or I pause cleaning?

Automatic cleanup pauses too. A note may pass the removal deadline while the Mac is asleep, offline, or paused. Decrumb reports an old or unconfirmed request instead of claiming success.

In the Mac app, you can request removal while paused. Each action attempts up to three requests; repeat it or resume cleaning if more remain queued. Manual removal does not turn incoming-message processing back on. On Pi, cleanup requests stay queued until you resume the worker.

What does Clear queued links do?

It discards links still waiting to be sent. It does not remove notes already in Signal.

Pause normally keeps valid queued links. Discard queued links on pause is optional and off by default. Saving changes to cleaning rules clears links queued under the old rules.

Update the app.

How do I get updates?

On Mac, choose Check for Updates… from the Decrumb menu. You can turn on automatic checks and installation in App updates; both are off by default.

Updates are verified before installation. Finish pairing and save or discard pending settings first. Decrumb reopens afterward and starts cleaning automatically, including if it was paused. Your linked account, rules, queued work, and receipts remain on your Mac.

If cleaning fails to start, choose Retry connection. RC1 users need one manual update to get the updater.

On Pi, download the new archive and rerun its installer as the same user. It preserves the linked account and settings. Pi upgrade instructions ↗

Your data and access.

Does Decrumb store messages?

The Signal client stores keys, contacts, account state, and some message data on your Mac, Pi, or Umbrel server. Decrumb also keeps a local queue of cleaned links and command replies waiting to be sent. That queue is not encrypted.

How do I disconnect it?

In Signal on your phone, open Linked Devices and unlink Decrumb’s device. Also pause Decrumb on the machine running it.

Unlinking does not erase local files or notes already delivered to your Signal devices. See the data removal details.

What’s still being tested?

RC6 is an early release for installation testing. Checks using real Signal accounts—for linking, message delivery, note removal, and starting at login—remain before stable v1. Physical Pi boot, logout, network recovery, and resource measurements also remain. See the current download.

Share a diagnostic report, if you choose.

In RC6 or later, open Diagnostics → Preview diagnostic report on Mac. Review the text, then choose Copy diagnostic report if you want to include it in a bug report. On Pi, run decrumb diagnostics. Umbrel has the same controls in its unlocked dashboard.

The report includes app and dependency versions, basic system information, local worker health, and recent error codes. It excludes messages, links, contacts, account details, cleaning rules, activity counts, and exact event times. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Clear diagnostics, or decrumb clear-diagnostics on Pi, removes local error history without changing your Signal connection, settings, queued links, or notes. New errors may be recorded afterward. A report without errors does not prove that no crash occurred.

Report a problem.

Tell us your operating system and Decrumb versions, what you expected, and what happened. For a link-cleaning problem, use a made-up example.

GitHub issues are public. Don’t include real messages, contacts, private links, QR codes, credentials, or the app’s data folder. Remove personal details from screenshots.

Using Decrumb on Umbrel.

Add the Decrumb community store in Umbrel’s App Store, install Decrumb, and open its browser dashboard. Use the app password displayed by Umbrel, then scan the Signal QR from your phone. Full setup guide ↗

Closing the browser leaves cleaning running. Pause stays in effect across browser refreshes and server restarts until you choose Start cleaning. Phone commands are optional and start off. If you run Decrumb on another machine, pause it before using Umbrel to avoid duplicate notes.

If initial setup fails, check internet access and allow at least 1 GB of free disk space, then choose Retry setup. Connection failures retry automatically. Update the app through Umbrel; its separate account state and settings are kept.
