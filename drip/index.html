One-tap logging

Tap the drop. Counter goes up. Under 100ms. Haptic feedback confirms the tap so you never have to look twice.

Daily goal

Default: 8 glasses. Set yours from 1 to 20 in settings. When you hit it, the bar turns green and a checkmark appears.

History

Scroll through your past days. See counts, goals, and glass sizes. No charts, no analytics. Just the numbers.

Undo

Accidental tap? An undo button appears for 5 seconds after every log. One tap to fix it.

Glass size & units

Adjust your glass size (1-32 oz) and switch between oz and mL. Changes take effect immediately.

Midnight reset

Each day starts fresh at midnight, local time. No carryover. The counter resets whether you're in the app or not.
