Drip

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 13, 2026

Short version: Drip does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

What Drip Is

Drip is a water tracking app for iOS and Android. You tap a button to log glasses of water, set a daily goal, and see your history. That's it.

Data We Collect

None. Drip collects zero personal data. The app does not:

  • Require an account, email address, or any personal information
  • Track your location
  • Access your contacts, photos, or other device data
  • Use cookies, analytics, or tracking pixels
  • Send any data to any server

Data Stored on Your Device

Drip stores the following data locally on your device using AsyncStorage:

  • Water entries: Daily glass counts, goal, and glass size for each day you use the app
  • Settings: Your preferred glass size, daily goal, and unit preference (oz or mL)

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, synced, backed up to any cloud service, or shared with anyone. If you delete the app, this data is permanently deleted.

Third-Party Services

Drip uses zero third-party services. There are no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting tools, no advertising networks, and no social media integrations. The app makes zero network requests during normal operation.

Network Requests

Drip makes zero network requests. The app functions identically whether your device is online or offline. All fonts and assets are bundled within the app. There are no remote dependencies.

Children's Privacy

Drip does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. The app does not require any personal information to use.

Data Retention

Your water tracking data is stored on your device for as long as the app is installed. We do not retain any data because we never receive any data. Deleting the app permanently removes all stored data.

Your Rights

Since Drip does not collect or process any personal data, standard data rights requests (access, deletion, portability) do not apply. Your data is already entirely under your control on your own device.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated date. Since Drip does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly. We recommend checking this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us at the contact information listed on our App Store or Google Play listing.
