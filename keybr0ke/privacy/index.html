Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Keybr0ke transforms text on your device. This policy describes the app’s current text processing and local settings.

Text you enter

Keybr0ke changes capitalization and substitutes some letters with numbers on your device. The current app does not send the text you enter or the transformed result to a server.

If you choose Copy, iOS places the result on the system clipboard. If you choose Share, the iOS share sheet passes it to the destination you select. Those destinations handle the information under their own privacy practices.

Settings and device storage

Your transformation percentages and appearance preference are saved in the app’s local storage on your device. Keybr0ke does not require an account, and the app has no in-app analytics feature.

Device backups and operating-system services are managed by Apple under your device settings and Apple’s privacy information.

Changes to the app

This policy describes the current app functionality. If a future version adds an account, analytics, or network features that change how information is handled, this policy will be updated.
