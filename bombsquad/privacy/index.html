Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's what we collect (spoiler: almost nothing).

Last updated: January 27, 2026

What We Collect

We only collect:

  • Username - An anonymous username you choose (no real name required)
  • Email - For account recovery
  • Puzzle times - Your completion times for puzzles

What We Don't Collect

We do not collect:

  • Location data
  • Tracking or analytics data

Data Sharing

We don't sell or share your data with anyone. Your puzzle times appear on leaderboards with your username only.

Delete Your Data

You can delete your account anytime from the app settings. This permanently removes all your data.

For privacy questions or support, contact hello@bombsquad-game.com.
